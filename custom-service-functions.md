# Non-standard-CRUD service functions

Generated audit of `server/*/service` methods that fall outside the standard CRUD set
(`Create`/`Update`/`Search`/`FindByID`/`DeleteByID`/`UndeleteByID` + helpers `checkScope`,
`scopeServices`, `load*`, `toLabeled*`, `recordAction`, constructors, error funcs).

**Totals:** 683 methods across 59 service types — 553 custom, 130 hooks.

> Two families are generated boilerplate, not hand-written logic:
> - `accessControl` (`access_control.gen.go`): `CanXxx` RBAC checks + `Effective/Trace/Resources/List/Grant/FindRules`.
> - federation `Sync`: `CreateRecord/UpdateRecord/DeleteRecord/FindRecords/Create|UpdateSharedModule` delegate to real CRUD.
